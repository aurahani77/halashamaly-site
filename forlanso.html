Teaching AI engines what Forlanso really is

How a GEO/AIO strategy moved Forlanso, across Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT and Perplexity, from a “freelance marketplace like Upwork” to a Remote Workforce Management System.

ClientForlanso
MarketUAE · Arab world
RoleSenior GEO & AI Search Strategist
PeriodMay 2026 – Present
01 · The challenge

AI engines were putting Forlanso in the wrong category

Forlanso is a platform founded in Dubai in 2021 that manages remote work end to end: digital contracts, payroll, payments and payouts in 150 countries. Yet AI assistants often grouped it with freelance marketplaces such as Upwork, Fiverr or Khamsat.

As buyers ask AI tools for recommendations instead of searching Google, that mistake meant Forlanso was compared against the wrong competitors and left out of the right questions.

How AI saw it

Freelance marketplace

A middleman connecting clients with freelancers, comparable to Upwork.

→
The real positioning

Remote Workforce Management System

Contracts, payroll, payments and team management, with 0% commission on operations.

02 · The approach

A 4-week GEO/AIO framework

Week 1

GEO foundations

Entity definition, category language and AI-readable brand facts.

Week 2

AI audit

Prompt testing across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini to map how each engine described the brand.

Week 3

Content strategy

Quote-ready content built around differentiators and conversational queries.

Week 4

Automation & tools

Workflows and tools for ongoing AI visibility tracking.

03 · What I did

Four moves that changed the answer

01

Entity & category clarity

Defined one consistent brand entity, “Remote Workforce Management System”, and reinforced it across owned and third-party sources.

02

Quote-ready content

Answer-first, fact-dense content LLMs can lift directly, built around 0% commission, payouts in 150 countries and compliant contracts.

03

Prompt & query mapping

Targeted the Arabic and English questions buyers ask AI about remote teams, global payroll and hiring in emerging markets.

04

Differentiation framing

An explicit “not a freelance marketplace” contrast, so AI engines learn the distinction and repeat it.

From “another freelance site” to a defined category

Category ownership

Every major engine now separates Forlanso from Upwork, Mostaql and Khamsat instead of grouping it with them.

Differentiators carried through

0% commission, the subscription model, payouts in 150 countries and legal contracts now appear in AI answers.

Owned-source citations

AI engines pull from forlanso.com, including the Arabic About page, and LinkedIn, so the brand controls the narrative.

Better follow-up prompts

Perplexity now suggests buyer-intent follow-ups such as “How do Forlanso subscriptions work instead of commissions?”

06 · Next phase

Where we go from here

  • Win comparison prompts such as “Forlanso vs Upwork” and “best remote payroll platform in the Arab world”.
  • Extend the same entity clarity to English-language AI queries and Google AI Overviews.
  • Track citation share and positioning accuracy across engines every month.
Key takeaway

In AI search, being found is not enough. You have to be understood. Clear entity definition plus quote-ready content changed the category AI assistants use to describe Forlanso.
