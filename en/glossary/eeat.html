E-E-A-T Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

A framework Google uses in its search quality rater guidelines to describe what it values in content.

Definition

A framework Google uses in its search quality rater guidelines to describe what it values in content. It is not a direct ranking factor by itself, but it guides how trustworthy content is built.

Why it matters

A framework that describes what Google values in trustworthy content. It is not a measurable score or a direct ranking factor, but it guides how pages worth citing are built.

What to do

  • Show the author and their real experience.
  • Cite your sources and show real experience, such as case studies.
  • Provide clear contact and ownership information.
