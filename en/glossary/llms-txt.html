llms.txt llms.txt file

A proposed text file placed at the site root that introduces language models to the site and its key pages.

Definition

A proposed text file placed at the site root that introduces language models to the site and its key pages. It is a proposal, not an official standard, and the major companies have not officially confirmed they use it.

Why it matters

It is a proposal, not an official standard, and the major companies have not officially confirmed they use it as a signal. Its first benefit is to you: it forces you to write a clear definition and pick your most important pages.

What to do

  • Write a two-sentence summary and links to your five to ten most important pages.
  • Upload it to the site root and update it when something substantial changes.

Related terms

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

The work of making your brand's content understood and trusted by generative answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, so they mention it or cite it in their answers.

Entity

Entity

A specific, well-known thing that engines can recognize and tell apart from others, such as a company, person, product or place.

RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

An approach where the tool first retrieves relevant pages or documents, then the model writes its answer based on them.
