Schema Markup

Code added to a page, usually in JSON-LD with schema.org vocabulary, that tells engines exactly what kind of content it is, such as a product, article, FAQ or organization.

Definition

Code added to a page, usually in JSON-LD with schema.org vocabulary, that tells engines exactly what kind of content it is, such as a product, article, FAQ or organization.

Why it matters

It removes ambiguity about what a page is and what it contains, and helps engines understand the relationships between its elements.

What to do

  • Choose the right type for your content: Organization, Article, FAQPage or Product.
  • Validate the code with testing tools before publishing, and do not add data that does not appear on the page.

Related terms

Entity

Entity

A specific, well-known thing that engines can recognize and tell apart from others, such as a company, person, product or place.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Optimizing content so it is chosen as the direct answer in short-form answers, whether Google snippets, voice assistants or AI tools.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

A set of practices for raising a site's ranking in search engines such as Google, covering content, technical structure, links and user experience.
