AEO Answer Engine Optimization

Optimizing content so it is chosen as the direct answer in short-form answers, whether Google snippets, voice assistants or AI tools.

Definition

Optimizing content so it is chosen as the direct answer in short-form answers, whether Google snippets, voice assistants or AI tools. It overlaps heavily with GEO and is sometimes used to mean the same thing.

Why it matters

AEO is usually used for short answers in search and voice assistants, while GEO focuses on conversational generative answer tools. The practices are very close.

What to do

  • Under every common question, write a direct two-sentence answer, then expand after it.
  • Add FAQPage structured data for the questions your page actually answers.

Related terms

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

The work of making your brand's content understood and trusted by generative answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, so they mention it or cite it in their answers.

Schema Markup

Schema Markup

Code added to a page, usually in JSON-LD with schema.org vocabulary, that tells engines exactly what kind of content it is, such as a product, article, FAQ or organization.

Zero-click Search

Zero-click Search

When a user gets their answer inside the search page or the AI tool without clicking any site.
