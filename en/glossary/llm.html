Glossary · Basics

LLM Large Language Model

An AI system trained on huge amounts of text to understand and generate language.

Definition

An AI system trained on huge amounts of text to understand and generate language. It is the engine behind ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and others.

Why it matters

A language model generates text from patterns it learned from huge datasets, and it can make mistakes or describe brands inaccurately. That is why your brand description needs to be fixed and consistent everywhere.

What to do

  • Test how more than one model describes you, and do not rely on a single answer.
  • Correct wrong information at its source: your site, your profiles and the sources that mention you.

Related terms

RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

An approach where the tool first retrieves relevant pages or documents, then the model writes its answer based on them.

Entity

Entity

A specific, well-known thing that engines can recognize and tell apart from others, such as a company, person, product or place.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

The work of making your brand's content understood and trusted by generative answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, so they mention it or cite it in their answers.
