Point Market: a search presence for a chain that grew from one branch to 37

How I rebuilt the website, set up an automated blog flow, ran SEO and GEO for the brand, and manage Google Maps profiles and branch-level marketing for all 37 stores.

ClientPoint Market · Grocery chain
MarketRiyadh & Jeddah
RoleDigital Marketing & SEO Lead
01 · Context

A chain that went from one branch to 37

Point Market is a grocery chain in Riyadh and Jeddah. It grew from one branch in 2023 to 37 today.

Growth at that pace puts two demands on marketing: every branch has to be easy to find on Google Maps, and the brand needs a website and content that search engines and AI tools can understand.

02 · What I did

Four workstreams, one brand

01

A new website

Built the brand's new website as the foundation for search.

02

Automated blog

Set up a publishing flow so blog content goes out automatically.

03

SEO + GEO

Ran SEO and GEO across the brand, so content is easy to find and easy to cite.

04

37 stores, locally

Manage the Google Maps profile and a branch-level marketing plan for every store, plus paid campaigns and budgets.

03 · At a glance
1 → 37Branches, from 2023 to today
37Google Maps profiles managed
4Workstreams: website, blog, SEO and GEO, local and paid
Key takeaway

For a multi-branch brand, local visibility is won branch by branch. Every store needs its own accurate, active Google Maps profile and plan, on top of a brand website that search engines and AI tools can understand.
