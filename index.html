GEO, answered

Questions brands ask me

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of structuring a brand's content, entities and technical signals so that AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand, trust and cite it in their answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank pages in a list of links. GEO aims to get your brand quoted inside the AI-generated answer itself. GEO builds on SEO foundations and adds entity clarity, quote-ready content, schema, llms.txt and AI citation tracking.

Do you work with Arabic content?

Yes. I'm a native Arabic speaker with C2 English and build bilingual GEO and SEO strategies for GCC and MENA brands.
