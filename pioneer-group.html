Pioneer Group: website content and a blog for a China sourcing agent

How I wrote the English content for every page of a Yiwu-based sourcing agent serving wholesale buyers, and built its blog with pillar guides and supporting articles on sourcing, inspection and shipping.

ClientPioneer Group · China sourcing agent
MarketYiwu, China · Wholesale buyers
RoleWebsite content & blog
01 · Context

A sourcing agent for wholesale buyers

Pioneer Group is a sourcing agent based in Yiwu, China, serving wholesale buyers.

Its audience is B2B: buyers who need clear answers about how sourcing, inspection and shipping work before they place an order. The website has to explain the service plainly and answer those questions.

02 · What I did

Four pieces of one content system

01

Every page in English

Wrote the English content for every page of the website.

02

Pillar and cluster blog

Built the blog around pillar guides, each backed by supporting articles.

03

Buyer topics

Covered the topics wholesale buyers ask about: sourcing, inspection and shipping from Yiwu.

04

Built for search

Linked pillar guides and supporting articles so each topic is covered in depth and easy to navigate.

03 · At a glance
100%Of website pages written in English
2-tierBlog: pillar guides plus supporting articles
B2BWholesale buyers as the audience
Key takeaway

For a B2B service, content does the first round of selling. Clear pages and in-depth guides answer a buyer's questions before they ever talk to a person.
