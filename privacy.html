Privacy

Privacy notice

What I collect, why, and how to ask me to delete it.

What I collect

When you submit the form after using a free tool, I collect your name, email and website, your WhatsApp number if you add it, your result in the tool (how many checks you completed and which ones you did not), and the page language.

Why

To email you the result and a short plan, and to contact you about it. I do not sell your data or share it with marketing companies.

The site scan

If you use the optional scan, my server opens the public pages of the website you enter (homepage, robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt) to check them. It does not store the pages or the result unless you submit the form.

Where it is stored

In a private spreadsheet on my Google account. Your answers inside the tool stay in your browser only and are sent only if you submit the form.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it, or up to 24 months after our last contact, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing halashamali77@gmail.com.
