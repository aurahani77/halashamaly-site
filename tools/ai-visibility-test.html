Common questions

Does the tool ask ChatGPT for me?

No. You open the question yourself, see the answer, and record it here. That way you see exactly what your customer sees.

Why can the answer change from one try to the next?

AI answers are not fixed. They can differ by day, by country and by person. Treat the result as a snapshot, and repeat it in a month.

Is my data saved?

Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you press the button to send me your details.

What if my business is not mentioned?

That is common, and it can change. The result gives you simple steps to start with.

How long does it take?

About two minutes for every three questions.
