How it works

  1. 1Scan your site (optional) and 8 technical checks are filled in for you.
  2. 2Tick what you have already done. Each check has a step and a label.
  3. 3See your score and your next 5 steps, ordered by impact.
  4. 4Repeat every month. AI answers change, so the score is a trend.

Your answers stay in this browser only. Nothing is sent unless you choose to email yourself the result.

Common questions

Does this tool use AI?

No. The score is simple weighted logic. The optional scan reads your public pages. It does not ask ChatGPT or any AI tool anything.

What do Documented and Practice mean?

Documented means the platform's own documentation says it. Practice means it comes from real projects or industry practice, and the platforms have not confirmed it. I keep them apart on purpose.

Does a high score guarantee that AI will cite me?

No. AI answers change and differ by country and by user. The checklist removes reasons not to be cited and makes your brand easier to understand. It cannot promise a result.

What does the scan read, and is anything stored?

Only the public homepage, robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt of the website you enter. Nothing is stored unless you submit the email form.

How often should I repeat it?

Once a month, together with a test of your real customer questions in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode.
