Qoyod: from search results to AI answers

How I re-engineered Qoyod's accounting templates and content for Generative Engine Optimization, so Google ranked them in under 24 hours and ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode started recommending Qoyod by name.

ClientQoyod · Cloud accounting SaaS
MarketSaudi Arabia
RoleSEO & GEO Content Strategy Lead
PeriodAug 2024 – Present
01 · The challenge

Great accounting content that AI couldn't quote

Qoyod's blog was rich in accounting knowledge, but it read as long-form articles. Generative engines struggled to summarize it or lift a clear, definitive answer from it.

The problem

Slow time-to-index

New content took weeks to surface, and long articles gave AI engines nothing concise to cite.

→
The opportunity

Templates as instant answers

Accounting templates are exactly what users want right now. Built well, they become fast lead magnets and citable knowledge assets.

02 · The strategic solution

GEO applied through three pillars

Pillar 1

Quote-ready architecture

Rewrote article intros and summaries to be concise and direct, so ChatGPT and Gemini can quote them as definitions. Turned complex data into data-driven lists and tables.

Pillar 2

Template engineering

Designed each template page as a knowledge asset, not just a download page, with Schema markup that tells Google it is a tool, not plain text.

Pillar 3

Indexing optimization

Linked new content into a strong internal linking structure so AI crawlers and search bots reach it the moment it is published.

03 · The results

Ranked in hours. Cited by AI.

<24hfrom publishing to page one, ahead of traditional competitors
#1position for 10+ high-intent accounting template keywords within one day
100%faster indexing than the site's average article, cutting weeks down to under 24 hours

Zero-lag indexing

Technical content indexed almost instantly, thanks to improved metadata and structured data.

AI citability

Qoyod content now appears as the model answer (featured snippet) for direct accounting questions, lifting its topical authority.

Generative engine visibility

Qoyod appears as a cited answer for specialized accounting terms in Perplexity and Gemini, at an estimated rate of up to 70%.

Search Console scale

3.1M clicks and 108.3M impressions over 16 months of Google Search Console data.

Templates built as knowledge assets, with quote-ready copy and clean structure, win twice: they rank in hours, and AI engines recommend the brand behind them.
